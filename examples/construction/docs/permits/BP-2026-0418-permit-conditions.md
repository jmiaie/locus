# Building Permit BP-2026-0418 — Conditions of Approval

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

Issued 2026-01-28 by the City of Port Ardell Building Division for a 4-storey, 92,000 sq ft medical office building at 1400 Harbor Point Way.

1. Special inspections required for cast-in-place concrete (strength testing and reinforcing placement), structural steel welding and high-strength bolting, and fire-resistive joints, per the approved Statement of Special Inspections.
2. Concrete strength test reports shall be submitted to the Building Division within 7 days of each test.
3. Deferred submittals: curtain wall, fire sprinkler, fire alarm and door hardware shall be approved before installation.
4. Construction work hours: Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. No work on Saturdays, Sundays or City holidays without written approval.
5. Truck haul route via Harbor Point Way and Route 9 only.
6. Stormwater: maintain inlet protection and the approved SWPPP at all times.
7. Fire marshal: doors in exit stairs shall not be equipped with delayed egress locking. Electrified locks on exit stair doors shall release on fire alarm activation and loss of power.
8. MRI suite: shielding and fire protection penetrations shall be inspected before ceiling cover.
9. Certificate of occupancy requires final fire marshal acceptance test of the sprinkler, fire alarm and door release systems.
