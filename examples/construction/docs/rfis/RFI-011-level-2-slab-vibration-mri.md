# RFI-011 — Level 2 slab vibration criterion (MRI suite)

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

**From:** Dana Whitfield, Granite & Vale Builders · **Date submitted:** 2026-03-02

## Question

The imaging tenant's MRI vendor site-planning guide requires a floor vibration criterion of 2,000 micro-inches per second at the magnet location. The Level 2 composite slab in 03 30 00 Rev 0 (4,000 psi, 6.25 inches) was not analysed for this criterion. The Suite 200 lease abstract lists an MRI-ready slab as a landlord delivery condition. Please confirm the slab design.

## Response (Meridian Structural Engineers, 2026-03-16)

Analysis shows the Rev 0 slab does not meet the criterion. Increase the Level 2 slab, gridlines C–F / 3–7, to 7.5 inches total thickness and f'c = 5,000 psi with fly ash limited to 20 percent and w/cm 0.42. Revised spec and drawings to be issued as ASI-004. Contractor to price the change.

## Disposition

Closed 2026-03-16. ASI-004 issued 2026-03-20 (03 30 00 Rev 1). Priced and executed as CO-002.
