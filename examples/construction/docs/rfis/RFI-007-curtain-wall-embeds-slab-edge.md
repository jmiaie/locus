# RFI-007 — Curtain wall embed plates vs. slab edge reinforcing

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

**From:** Dana Whitfield, Project Engineer, Granite & Vale Builders (GC)
**To:** Okafor Lindqvist Architects; Meridian Structural Engineers
**Date submitted:** 2026-02-03 · **Response required by:** 2026-02-10

## Question

At gridline A, Levels 2–4, the curtain wall embed plates shown on the curtain wall shop drawings (08 44 13) land on the two #5 edge bars and the hairpins detailed on S-201, detail 7. Spec 03 30 00 section 3.4 prohibits displacing reinforcing without approval. Please confirm the embed location or provide a revised detail.

## Response (Meridian Structural Engineers, 2026-02-10)

Shift embed plates 4 inches inboard from the slab edge. Add two #5 hairpins at each embed, lapped 24 inches. Edge bars remain as detailed. See sketch SK-S-07. No change to contract sum or time is anticipated.

## Disposition

Closed 2026-02-10. Incorporated into 03 30 00 Rev 1 section 3.4 by reference.
