# RFI-021 — Low 28-day cylinder breaks, Level 2 slab pour of 2026-04-09

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

**From:** Dana Whitfield, Granite & Vale Builders · **Date submitted:** 2026-05-08

## Question

The 28-day cylinder results for the Level 2 slab pour of 2026-04-09 (mix L2-5000 per SUB-03-001 Rev B) average 4,620 psi against the specified f'c of 5,000 psi (03 30 00 Rev 1). Two of three tests are more than 500 psi below f'c (special inspection report SI-0412). Please advise on acceptance.

## Response (Meridian Structural Engineers, 2026-05-19)

Take three cores per ASTM C42 from the affected area per ACI 318 26.12.6. Concrete will be considered structurally adequate if the average of three cores is at least 85 percent of f'c and no single core is below 75 percent of f'c. Coring and testing at the Contractor's cost.

## Follow-up (2026-05-29)

Cores averaged 5,180 psi (lowest 4,960 psi). Concrete accepted. No change to contract sum. Testing cost borne by the Contractor.
