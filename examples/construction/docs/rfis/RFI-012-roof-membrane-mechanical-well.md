# RFI-012 — Roof membrane durability at the mechanical well

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

**From:** Luis Ortega, Roofing Foreman, Summit Roofing (subcontractor), via Granite & Vale Builders · **Date submitted:** 2026-04-14

## Question

The mechanical well will see frequent service traffic for the MRI chillers. A 60-mil mechanically attached TPO (07 54 23 Rev 0) has a history of punctures and fastener back-out in high-traffic areas. Please confirm whether the owner wants an upgraded membrane before roofing materials are released.

## Response (Okafor Lindqvist Architects, 2026-04-24)

Owner (Harbor Point Health Holdings) accepts the recommendation. Change the field membrane to 80-mil fully adhered TPO over 1/2-inch gypsum cover board, upgrade the warranty to 30-year NDL, add walkway pads at the mechanical well. To be issued as ASI-009; Contractor to submit a change order proposal.

## Disposition

Closed 2026-04-24. ASI-009 issued 2026-05-04 (07 54 23 Rev 1). Executed as CO-003.
