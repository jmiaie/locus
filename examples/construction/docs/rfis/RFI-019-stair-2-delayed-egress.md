# RFI-019 — Stair 2 door hardware: delayed egress vs. permit condition 7

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

**From:** Dana Whitfield, Granite & Vale Builders · **Date submitted:** 2026-05-27

## Question

Hardware set HW-5 (08 71 00 Rev 0) specifies an electromagnetic lock with 15-second delayed egress, fail-secure, at the Stair 2 door into the Level 2 imaging suite. Building permit BP-2026-0418 condition 7 states that doors in exit stairs may not have delayed egress and electrified locks must release on fire alarm. During the 2026-05-26 site walk the deputy fire marshal said the HW-5 set would not pass final inspection. Please advise.

## Response (Okafor Lindqvist Architects, 2026-06-03)

Delete delayed egress from HW-5. Provide exit device with access control from the stair side only, free egress at all times, fail-safe on loss of power or fire alarm. To be issued as ASI-011. The tenant's security consultant has accepted card-reader control on the stair side.

## Disposition

Closed 2026-06-03. ASI-011 issued 2026-06-08 (08 71 00 Rev 1). Cost included in CO-005.
