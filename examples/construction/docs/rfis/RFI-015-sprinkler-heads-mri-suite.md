# RFI-015 — Sprinkler heads and piping in the MRI scan room

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

**From:** Priya Raman, Fire Protection Coordinator, Bayline Fire Protection, via Granite & Vale Builders · **Date submitted:** 2026-05-05

## Question

Section 21 13 13 section 2.3 calls for non-ferrous piping and sprinklers in Zone IV but does not define the extent. The shielding vendor's drawings show the 5-gauss line extending 3 feet outside the scan room into the control room. Does the non-ferrous requirement extend to the 5-gauss line?

## Response (Okafor Lindqvist Architects with the MRI physicist, 2026-05-12)

Yes. Use copper piping and brass-bodied quick-response sprinklers everywhere inside the 5-gauss line, including the control room ceiling. Penetrations through the RF shield use waveguides supplied by the shielding vendor. Contractor to price the added copper piping.

## Disposition

Closed 2026-05-12. Priced in CO-005.
