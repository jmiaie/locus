# SUB-07-004 — TPO roofing product data and warranty

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

**Subcontractor:** Summit Roofing · **Submitted by:** Granite & Vale Builders

## Revision A — reviewed 2026-05-12: Rejected

Submitted 60-mil mechanically attached TPO with 20-year warranty. Rejected: does not comply with 07 54 23 Rev 1 (ASI-009, issued 2026-05-04), which requires 80-mil fully adhered membrane, cover board and a 30-year NDL warranty.

## Revision B — reviewed 2026-05-26: Approved

80-mil fully adhered TPO, 1/2-inch gypsum cover board, tapered polyiso average R-33, manufacturer's 30-year NDL warranty letter of intent. Approved.
