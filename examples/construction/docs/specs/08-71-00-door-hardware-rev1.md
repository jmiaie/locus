# Section 08 71 00 — Door Hardware (Rev 1)

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

Revision 1 issued 2026-06-08 by ASI-011 in response to RFI-019 and building permit condition 7 (fire marshal).

## 2.4 Hardware Sets

- HW-1 Tenant entry: storeroom lockset, card reader, closer.
- HW-3 MRI suite (Zone IV): non-magnetic hardware, aluminum or brass only, no ferrous components.
- HW-5 Stair 2 at Level 2: exit device with card-reader access control from the stair side only; delayed egress deleted; free egress at all times; fail-safe on loss of power or fire alarm. [REV 1]
- HW-6 Stair 1: exit device, closer, no electrified locking.

## 3.6 Coordination

Electrified hardware at exit stairs must release on fire alarm per permit BP-2026-0418 condition 7.
