# Section 07 54 23 — TPO Roofing (Rev 1)

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

Revision 1 issued 2026-05-04 by ASI-009 following RFI-012 (membrane durability at the mechanical well). Priced in CO-003.

## 1.6 Warranty

Manufacturer's 30-year no-dollar-limit (NDL) system warranty. [REV 1]

## 2.1 Membrane

- Thermoplastic polyolefin sheet, ASTM D6878, 80-mil nominal thickness, white. [REV 1]
- Attachment: fully adhered with low-VOC bonding adhesive over cover board. [REV 1]

## 2.3 Roof Insulation

Tapered polyisocyanurate, minimum average R-33, plus 1/2-inch gypsum cover board. [REV 1]

## 3.5 Rooftop Equipment Curbs

Flash all rooftop unit curbs and the mechanical well with membrane turned up minimum 8 inches; add walkway pads around the mechanical well.
