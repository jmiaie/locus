# Section 03 30 00 — Cast-in-Place Concrete (Rev 1)

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

Revision 1 issued 2026-03-20 by ASI-004 in response to RFI-011 (Level 2 MRI vibration criterion). Changes from Rev 0 are marked [REV 1].

## 1.3 Performance Requirements

- Elevated slab, Level 2 (MRI suite and imaging tenant area, gridlines C–F / 3–7): f'c = 5,000 psi at 28 days. [REV 1]
- Other elevated slabs: f'c = 4,000 psi at 28 days (unchanged).
- Columns and shear walls: f'c = 5,000 psi at 28 days.
- Maximum water-cementitious materials ratio 0.42 for the Level 2 slab mix. [REV 1]
- Fly ash (ASTM C618 Class F) limited to 20 percent of cementitious materials in the Level 2 slab mix. [REV 1]
- Slump at point of placement 4 inches, plus or minus 1 inch, before high-range water reducer.

## 2.2 Elevated Slab Thickness

Level 2 slab, gridlines C–F / 3–7: total thickness 7.5 inches (3-inch deck plus 4.5-inch topping) to meet the floor vibration criterion of 2,000 micro-inches per second for the MRI suite. [REV 1] Other slabs: 6.25 inches.

## 3.4 Embedded Items

Coordinate curtain wall embed plates at slab edges with reinforcing shown on S-201 and the RFI-007 response detail.

## 3.9 Field Quality Control

- Strength tests per ASTM C39: one set of four cylinders per 50 cubic yards of each mix placed in any one day.
- Acceptance per ACI 318 Section 26.12.
- Low-strength results: investigate per ACI 318 26.12.6 (cores per ASTM C42).
