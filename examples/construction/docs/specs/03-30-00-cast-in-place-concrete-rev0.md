# Section 03 30 00 — Cast-in-Place Concrete (Rev 0)

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

Issued for Construction 2026-01-12 by Okafor Lindqvist Architects with Meridian Structural Engineers.

## 1.3 Performance Requirements

- Elevated slabs on composite metal deck, all levels: specified compressive strength f'c = 4,000 psi at 28 days.
- Columns and shear walls: f'c = 5,000 psi at 28 days.
- Footings and grade beams: f'c = 4,000 psi at 28 days.
- Maximum water-cementitious materials ratio 0.45 for elevated slabs.
- Fly ash (ASTM C618 Class F) may replace up to 25 percent of cementitious materials by weight.
- Slump at point of placement 4 inches, plus or minus 1 inch, before high-range water reducer.

## 2.2 Elevated Slab Thickness

Elevated slab total thickness 6.25 inches (3-inch deck plus 3.25-inch topping) unless noted on S-201.

## 3.4 Embedded Items

Coordinate curtain wall embed plates at slab edges with reinforcing shown on S-201 before placement. Do not cut or displace reinforcing to install embeds without the Structural Engineer's written approval.

## 3.9 Field Quality Control

- Strength tests per ASTM C39: one set of four cylinders per 50 cubic yards, or fraction, of each mix placed in any one day.
- Acceptance per ACI 318 Section 26.12: average of any three consecutive tests at least f'c, and no single test more than 500 psi below f'c.
- Low-strength results: investigate per ACI 318 26.12.6 (cores per ASTM C42) at the Contractor's expense if cores confirm deficiency.
