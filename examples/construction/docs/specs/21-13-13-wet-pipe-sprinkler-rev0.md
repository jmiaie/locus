# Section 21 13 13 — Wet-Pipe Sprinkler Systems (Rev 0)

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

## 1.4 Design Criteria

- NFPA 13, edition adopted by the City of Port Ardell.
- Medical office and imaging areas: Light Hazard; MRI equipment and mechanical rooms: Ordinary Hazard Group 1.
- Quick-response sprinklers throughout.

## 2.3 MRI Suite

Within the MRI scan room (Zone IV) use non-ferrous piping and sprinklers. Coordinate penetrations of the RF shield with the shielding vendor; use waveguides at all shield penetrations.

## 3.2 Clearances

Maintain minimum 18 inches clearance below sprinkler deflectors to storage and equipment.
