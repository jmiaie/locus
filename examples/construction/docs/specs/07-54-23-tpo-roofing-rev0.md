# Section 07 54 23 — TPO Roofing (Rev 0)

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT.** Fictional project, companies, people and figures, written for the Locus construction demo. Not engineering, legal or code advice.

## 1.6 Warranty

Manufacturer's 20-year no-dollar-limit (NDL) system warranty covering membrane, insulation, flashings and accessories.

## 2.1 Membrane

- Thermoplastic polyolefin sheet, ASTM D6878, 60-mil nominal thickness, white.
- Attachment: mechanically attached in field, perimeter and corner zones per FM Global 1-90 rating.

## 2.3 Roof Insulation

Tapered polyisocyanurate, minimum average R-30, with 1/4 inch per foot slope to drains and crickets.

## 3.5 Rooftop Equipment Curbs

Flash all rooftop unit curbs and the mechanical well with membrane turned up minimum 8 inches above finished roof surface.
